# My Consulting Template Library

I use these templates to keep my Project Northstar work structured and traceable. I copy the relevant files into `deliverables/working/week_1/` and update them as my evidence and conclusions develop.

| Template | When I use it | What it helps me achieve |
|---|---|---|
| `workplan_template.md` | All four weeks | I manage scope, dependencies, and deadlines |
| `issue_tree_template.md` | Weeks 1–2 | I connect questions, hypotheses, and evidence |
| `analysis_log_template.md` | Weeks 1–3 | I make my calculations reproducible |
| `findings_log_template.md` | Weeks 1–4 | I turn facts into implications and actions |
| `decision_log_template.md` | All four weeks | I preserve decisions and follow-ups |
| `interview_note_template.md` | Stakeholder work | I separate statements from verified facts |
| `source_log.csv` | Research and drafting | I cite claims and record limitations |
| `assumptions_register.csv` | Analysis and business case | I make uncertainty visible and testable |
| `risk_register.csv` | Diagnosis and planning | I assign risk mitigation and ownership |
| `maturity_assessment_template.csv` | Week 2 | I score capabilities consistently |
| `initiative_charter_template.md` | Week 4 | I make the roadmap executable |
| `executive_deck_storyboard.md` | Weeks 3–4 | I build an answer-first narrative |
| `recommendation_memo_template.md` | Week 4 | I record my final recommendation |
| `weekly_update_template.md` | Every week | I communicate progress, risks, and next steps |

Together, these files create the evidence trail behind my final deck, memo, business case, roadmap, and KPI framework. At the end of Week 4, they also support a clear handoff to ACG.
