# Your Consulting Template Library

Copy these templates into `deliverables/working/week_1/` when you begin. Keep them current throughout the engagement. A polished final deck cannot compensate for an undocumented analytical process.

| Template | When you use it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `workplan_template.md` | Throughout the project | Controls scope, dependencies, and deadlines |
| `issue_tree_template.md` | Days 2–10 | Links questions, hypotheses, evidence, and decisions |
| `analysis_log_template.md` | Days 4–17 | Makes calculations reproducible |
| `findings_log_template.md` | Days 5–17 | Converts facts into implications and actions |
| `decision_log_template.md` | Entire engagement | Prevents alignment from being lost |
| `interview_note_template.md` | Stakeholder work | Separates statements from verified facts |
| `source_log.csv` | Research and slides | Supports accurate citation and limitations |
| `assumptions_register.csv` | Analysis and business case | Makes uncertainty visible and testable |
| `risk_register.csv` | Delivery and roadmap | Creates ownership of mitigation |
| `maturity_assessment_template.csv` | Week 2 | Creates consistent capability scoring |
| `initiative_charter_template.md` | Week 4 | Makes the roadmap executable |
| `executive_deck_storyboard.md` | Days 10 and 17 | Builds an answer-first narrative |
| `recommendation_memo_template.md` | Week 4 | Produces a concise written record |
| `weekly_update_template.md` | End of every week | Communicates progress, findings, risks, and next steps |
