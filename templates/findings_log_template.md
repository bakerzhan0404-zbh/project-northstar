## Promotion criteria

A finding can enter the core deck only if:

- The fact is supported and correctly scoped.
- The magnitude reconciles to the analytical baseline.
- The implication affects the client decision.
- Material uncertainty and counterevidence are visible.
- The source or reproducible analysis is identified.
