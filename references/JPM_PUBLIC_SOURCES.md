# JPMorgan Public Evidence Register

Last reviewed: 30 July 2026.

This register makes the JPMorgan connection substantive and auditable. Sources provide external context, benchmarks, terminology, and examples. They do **not** describe ACG, supply ACG data, or predetermine the recommendation.

For a learner-ready summary of the most useful figures, see [`JPM_BENCHMARK_PACK.md`](JPM_BENCHMARK_PACK.md).

## Evidence classification

- **JPM-PUBLIC:** A statistic, statement, framework, or client example published on an official JPMorgan domain.
- **ACG-DATA:** A fact from the ACG project dataset or client materials.
- **ANALYST-CALC:** Your reproducible calculation from the project data.
- **ANALYST-ASSUMPTION:** An explicitly labeled input requiring validation.
- **ANALYST-JUDGMENT:** Your recommendation or interpretation.

Every important slide claim should be classifiable into one of these categories.

## Core required reading

### S01 — Payments Outlook 2026: Five Shifts Powering Payments

- **Publisher:** J.P. Morgan Payments
- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/payments/trends-innovation/payments-outlook-trends-2026
- **PDF:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpmorgan/documents/payments/payments-outlook-five-shifts-powering-payments-2026-report.pdf
- **Use in Northstar:** Industry context for real-time liquidity, centralization, connected treasury, automation, data harmonization, fraud defense, and emerging payment infrastructure.
- **Public benchmarks available:** The report states that 87% of organizations have some automation in treasury/payments infrastructure, while 39% describe their systems as mostly or fully automated. It also reports that 92% of treasury professionals view embedded banking and finance capabilities as very or somewhat valuable.
- **Caution:** Confirm figures and footnotes in the live source before final submission. Do not treat survey observations as a performance target or causal proof.

### S02 — Fortescue Global Treasury Transformation

- **Publisher:** J.P. Morgan Payments
- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/treasury/liquidity-management/fortescue-global-treasury-transformation
- **Use in Northstar:** A current public example of treasury infrastructure responding to global scale and complexity.
- **Public facts useful for comparison:** The story describes Fortescue's global growth and a network of more than 180 bank accounts across multiple countries and currencies.
- **Caution:** This is a client success story, not an independent benchmark. Extract problem–intervention–outcome logic without assuming the same solution fits ACG.

### S03 — Ansys EMEA Treasury Transformation

- **Publisher:** J.P. Morgan Payments
- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/treasury/liquidity-management/emea-treasury-transformation-ansys
- **Use in Northstar:** Example of centralizing cash management and pursuing an automated liquidity structure across EMEA.
- **Caution:** Use to generate questions and design options, not to claim that centralization is automatically appropriate.

### S04 — Mettler-Toledo China Treasury Transformation

- **Publisher:** J.P. Morgan Payments
- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/treasury/treasury-management/mettler-toledo-china-treasury-transformation
- **Use in Northstar:** Example of digitization, automation, local-market complexity, and access to funding on an as-needed basis.
- **Caution:** Local regulation, tax, bank availability, and entity facts differ. You must not generalize a China structure to all ACG markets.

### S05 — ISO 20022 Migration and Payments Automation

- **Publisher:** J.P. Morgan
- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/treasury/treasury-management/what-is-iso-20022
- **Use in Northstar:** Explains how structured payment data can support reconciliation, screening, automation, and cross-border processing.
- **Public fact:** JPMorgan Chase states that it went live with ISO 20022 in March 2023.
- **Caution:** Northstar is not an ISO implementation project. You should translate the source into data and process implications, not a technical migration plan.

### S07 — Treasury and Liquidity Solutions Overview

- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/solutions/treasury/liquidity-and-account-solutions
- **Use:** Understand industry vocabulary around accounts, liquidity visibility, centralization, and working capital.
- **Caution:** This is product-oriented material. The consulting recommendation must start with client needs and design principles, not products.

### S08 — J.P. Morgan Payments Scale

- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/solutions/treasury/payments
- **Public figures displayed when reviewed:** More than $10 trillion processed daily, 120 currencies, 160 countries, and 40 payment methods; the page also states that 90% of the Fortune 500 choose J.P. Morgan for payment needs.
- **Use:** One optional context footnote on the scale and complexity of global payments.
- **Caution:** Verify the current page and its numbered footnotes before use. These figures do not measure ACG's opportunity.

### S09 — Working Capital Accelerator Announcement

- **Official link:** https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/introducing-working-capital-accelerator
- **Use:** Current illustration of the industry movement from fragmented reporting toward centralized, real-time working-capital insight.
- **Public context:** The announcement references JPMorganChase's $19.8 billion annual technology investment.
- **Caution:** A product announcement is not evidence of client benefit by itself.

### S10 — 2025 JPMorganChase Annual Report

- **Official page:** https://www.jpmorganchase.com/ir/annual-report
- **Use:** Firm-level context and clear distinction between Consumer & Community Banking, Commercial & Investment Bank, and Asset & Wealth Management.
- **Caution:** Use the correct business name. This is a J.P. Morgan Payments client advisory engagement, not a retail Chase or investment-banking assignment.

## How you should use these sources

1. Read the original page, including methodology and footnotes.
2. Capture the exact claim in `templates/source_log.csv`.
3. Record publication date, access date, evidence type, and limitations.
4. Paraphrase unless exact wording is essential.
5. Put a short source footnote on the slide and the full citation in the appendix.
6. Explain why the evidence is relevant to ACG.
7. Do not combine an external benchmark with ACG data in a way that obscures which source produced each figure.

## Recommended citation format

`J.P. Morgan Payments, “Payments Outlook: Five Trends Powering Payments in 2026,” 23 Apr. 2026, accessed 30 Jul. 2026.`

For the ACG project data:

`Source: Project Northstar ACG dataset; analyst calculation.`
