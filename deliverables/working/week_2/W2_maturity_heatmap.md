**Prepared by:** Baker

**Status:** Provisional; requires process-owner validation

**Classification:** Confidential — Project Northstar simulated client material

## Executive conclusion

ACG's limiting capability is not simply technology. Across the findings promoted in Week 2, **data ownership, process standardization, decision rights, and performance evidence are all at an emerging level**. A staged intervention around existing ERPs is testable only if governance and control design move with connectivity—not after it.

This heatmap satisfies the Week 2 maturity requirement without creating a false composite score. The dimensions are not equally weighted, no overall average is calculated, and each provisional score is tied to observable supplied evidence.

## Observable 1–5 scale

| Score | Observable state |
|---:|---|
| 1 — Ad hoc | Work depends on individuals; definitions and evidence are absent or inconsistent |
| 2 — Emerging | Repeatable local practices exist, but ownership, standards, integration, or measurement are fragmented |
| 3 — Defined | Enterprise policy, roles, definitions, and controls are documented and used consistently |
| 4 — Managed | Performance is measured, exceptions have owners, controls are tested, and decisions use reconciled data |
| 5 — Optimized | Capabilities improve through automated feedback, predictive insight, and independently evidenced benefits |

## Heatmap readout

- **Sequence governance with connectivity:** Technology is not the sole bottleneck; data ownership and decision rights are prerequisites.
- **Use two targeted pilots:** Delayed balance sources and extract-bounded payment cohorts provide testable scopes without forcing an ERP replacement.
- **Keep value provisional:** Liquidity, capacity, and fee outcomes remain scenarios until account mobility, observed time, removability, and source populations are certified.
- **Protect local context and control:** ACG needs defined local autonomy, critical-payment service levels, emergency procedures, resilience testing, and rollback before scaling.

## Validation questions

1. Who owns each cash, account, payment, counterparty, and restriction data object?
2. Which decisions remain local, which become enterprise policy, and which require dual approval?
3. What evidence demonstrates access, segregation, emergency-payment, and business-continuity control today?
4. Which KPIs already exist, who signs off the baseline, and what changes funding or rollout decisions?
5. Which acquired ERP is retiring, and how does that change the integration sequence?
