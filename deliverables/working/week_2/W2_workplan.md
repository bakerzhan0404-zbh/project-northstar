## Week 3 work sequence

1. Confirm the diagnostic and named evidence owners at the Treasurer checkpoint.
2. Write design principles before naming a preferred option.
3. Define evaluation criteria and weights before scoring options.
4. Build the future-state responsibility/control design for each credible option.
5. Build the business case from certified evidence; otherwise show validation cases and ranges.
6. Stress every option at $21m mobility, two closures, 50 hours/month, required controls, peak-season continuity, and four-hour rollback.
