## Week 3 scope and boundaries

**In scope, 17–23 August:** confirm the five findings and three evidence gates; define six-to-eight observable design principles; compare local stabilization, federated coordination, and globally coordinated options under one pre-agreed scoring model; draft the future-state cash-positioning and payment operating model, RACI, controls, service levels, and two pilot charters; keep cash release, P&L, capacity, and risk cases separate; and prepare the ten-slide CFO interim pack and Q&A.

**Analytical perimeter:** retain the reconciled Week 2 populations—55 accounts and 9,955 account-days from 1 January–30 June 2026; 7,600 supplied payment records and $198.14m gross translated payment-intent value; and nine management-estimated process activities. New evidence enters only after source/population reconciliation and an analysis-log update.

**Out of scope:** pilot execution or production change; bank, product, or vendor selection; ERP replacement or detailed implementation architecture; account closure or cash-transfer authorization; legal, tax, regulatory, sanctions, accounting, or credit advice; and funded value from mobility, labor, fees, receivables, or FX before the evidence owner and Finance approve the baseline.

## Success and decision criteria

### Week 3 design exits successfully when

1. The Treasurer confirms or records disagreement with the five findings; each decision-critical evidence package is reconciled or remains an explicit gate with owner, due date, and consequence.
2. Six-to-eight design principles have observable tests, and three genuinely comparable options cover organization, governance, process, data, technology, controls, service, cost, and what each deliberately does not solve.
3. Criteria and weights are locked before scoring; the preferred option survives—or has an explicit switching condition under—the $21m mobility, two-closure, and 50-hour downside cases while preserving controls, peak-season continuity, local emergency rights, and four-hour rollback.
4. Both pilot charters specify objective, population, selection rule, exclusions, KPI formulas/denominators, controlled baseline plan, proposed targets, owners, duration, cost range, controls/service gates, rollback, and scale/stop decision. Week 3 authorizes design only.
5. Cash release, P&L, capacity, and risk remain separate; uncertified mobility, capacity, fee, receivables, or FX value remains `$0` in the funded case.
6. Baker delivers the ten-slide interim deck and five CFO Q&A answers by 23 August so the CFO can choose a direction and state its conditions and deferrals.

### A later pilot can succeed only under approved charters

- Visibility: proposed threshold of at least 95% of expected pilot account-days by approved cutoff and 100% reconciled or formally explained for four consecutive weeks; zero critical service/control incidents; rollback drill at or below four hours.
- Payments: controlled population and value reconcile; exception rate and repair minutes per 100 improve against a pre-agreed like-for-like baseline; late release and rejection do not worsen; every reviewed issue has reason/owner/action evidence; critical service/control failures remain zero for four consecutive weeks. Quantitative improvement targets are set only after the controlled baseline.
- Pilot completion does not itself authorize scale, cash value, labor savings, or account closure. Treasurer/CIO/BU Finance make a separate scale decision.
