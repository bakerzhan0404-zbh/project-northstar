### Quantified-hypothesis convention

Bold cells combine observed `ANALYST-CALC` anchors with falsifiable `ANALYST-ASSUMPTION` tests. V1, V3, V4, V5, R1, R2, R3, R4, and F2 state supplied-data anchors; V2's mobility scenarios, R1's greater-than-$5m gate, F1's delivery targets, F3's realization range, and E3's evidence gate remain analyst tests. A $0 quantified evidence position for V5 or R4 means no benefit can currently be supported—not that the underlying opportunity is known to be zero. Week 2 must retain these denominators or record any definition change in the decision and analysis logs. E1 and E2 remain qualitative until process-owner, service-criticality, readiness, and peak-calendar evidence is available.

## Evidence discipline

Statuses are limited to `Untested`, `Supported`, `Weakened`, `Rejected`, and `Unresolved`. External cases generate questions and design hypotheses; only ACG evidence can establish the ACG recommendation.
