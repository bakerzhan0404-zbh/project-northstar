### Quantified-hypothesis convention

Every initial-hypothesis cell is bold and combines an observed `ANALYST-CALC` anchor with an intentionally ambitious, falsifiable `ANALYST-ASSUMPTION` target or acceptance gate. The fixed cross-branch stress case is $21m movable cash, two closures, and one-third of identified capacity; Week 2 must retain these thresholds or record any change in the decision and analysis logs. The $73.61k–$147.22k FX screen, $2.01m–$4.03m restriction-clearance cases, capacity targets, operating thresholds, and delivery gates are hypotheses—not findings or booked benefits.
