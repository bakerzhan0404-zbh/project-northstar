# Week 4 — Recommendation Risk Summary

The governing register is `W4_risk_register.csv`. Scores are likelihood × impact on a 1–5 scale and are analyst judgments, not quantified risk value.

## Priority risks

| Risk | Score | Why it can change the decision | Immediate control |
|---|---:|---|---|
| R033 Data/metric ownership not accepted | 20 | Prevents every later baseline, control, and value decision | G1 signed contract or switch/narrow |

| R038 Local rights lost | 15 | Makes the model operationally fragile | Explicit delegated and emergency rights |
| R039 Critical control failure | 15 | Blocks production regardless of value | Stop, contain, remediate, retest |
| R042 KPI/benefit drift | 15 | Manufactures apparent performance | Finance-owned definitions and restatement |

## Risk response rule

No high initiative score, financial hypothesis, or schedule pressure can compensate for a failed critical data, control, service, local-right, architecture, resilience, affordability, or rollback gate. The response is to stop, narrow, redesign, extend evidence with owners/dates, or switch to local stabilization.
