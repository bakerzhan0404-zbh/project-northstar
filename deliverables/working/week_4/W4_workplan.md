- One-time and recurring cost are separated.
- Every initiative has an outcome, owner, dependencies, gates, completion evidence, and stop/exit rule.
- Every KPI has definition/formula, source, cadence, owner, baseline boundary, and target logic.
- Every core slide has an answer-first title, traceable evidence, visible limitation, and decision implication.
- The final deck remains editable and passes rendered review.
