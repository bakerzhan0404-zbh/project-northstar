## 1. Why recommend federated coordination when the model has no funded value?

**Short answer:** Because the recommendation is about the safest, most useful direction for closing the evidence and operating-model gaps—not a claim that value is already proven.

The base comparison scores federated coordination `87`, local stabilization `72`, and globally coordinated `60`. Federated also leads all five plausible alternative-weight cases. It best combines one data/control/KPI spine with regional and local execution, exception, and emergency rights. That directly addresses the observed source, ownership, control, and decision-chain gaps without requiring immediate global centralization or ERP replacement.

This remains `ANALYST-JUDGMENT`. The scores are not dollars, confidence, cost, or execution-readiness measures. All execution-evidence gates remain `OPEN`, every current recognized cash/P&L/capacity value is `$0`, and risk is `NOT QUANTIFIED`.

**Decision implication:** Align on federated coordination only as the provisional detailed-design direction. Retain local stabilization as the fallback and do not authorize implementation, spend, or benefit recognition.

**Evidence:** `W3_strategic_options.md`; `W3_business_case.md`; A17–A19.

## 2. What does the `$38.13m` result mean, and why is movable cash still `$0`?

**Short answer:** `$38.13m` is the 30 June result of a 14-day diagnostic screen. It is not a certified surplus, forecast, transfer instruction, or funding benefit.

The screen subtracts preliminary restrictions/deficits and trailing supplied payment intent under an analyst-defined window. Across 168 complete windows, the `$21m` threshold passes `168/168`, `$35m` passes `138/168`, and `$46.2m` passes `0/168`. That supports further liquidity design, but the supplied data does not certify authoritative balance type, receipt timestamp, complete payroll/tax/seasonal needs, receipts, operating buffers, legal/local transferability, settlement calendars, facility use, transfer timing, or economics.

Until VG01–VG05 are closed and Finance recognizes an attributable result, validated, funded, and recognized cash value remains `$0`.

**Decision implication:** Authorize account-level evidence ownership and mobility certification work only. Do not move cash or book liquidity value from the screen.

**Evidence:** `W2_findings_log.md` F08; `W3_business_case.md`; `W3_assumptions_register.csv`.

## 4. Will the two pilot designs be safe and decision-useful, or are they just small samples?

**Short answer:** They are useful as bounded evidence designs only if the readiness, control, service, and rollback prerequisites close. Neither is authorized to launch.

The visibility design starts with an all-55-account / 9,955-account-day readiness census, then carries a purposive ten-account frame: five Spreadsheet and five Portal accounts spanning three regions, three ERP environments, and four banks. All 10 require base readiness/control review; APAC Payroll/restricted `AC0040` alone is enhanced-review and shadow-only.

The payment design selects 120 unique records, 30 per each of four mutually exclusive cohorts. Each cohort contains eight exception/status cases, seven late-only cases, and 15 issue-flag-negative controls whose supplied status is `Completed`. Across 60 pairs, 50 are exact on payment type, region, month, and amount band; 10 nearest-match deviations are documented. This is a purposive root-cause design—not a prevalence, causal, settlement, or benefit sample.

Safety depends on evidence outside the frames: authoritative sources and owners, source documents, criticality, reason codes and timestamps, SoD/access/control approval, critical-service and blackout rules, actual cost ranges, a rehearsed return to the approved prior process within four hours, and a separately recorded go/no-go.

**Decision implication:** Permit readiness and evidence-linkage work, not production intervention. Stop, substitute, or redesign if any critical control/service/local-right/comparability gate fails.

**Evidence:** `W3_visibility_pilot_charter.md`; `W3_payment_pilot_charter.md`; `W3_pilot_model_controls.csv`.

## 5. Does the recommendation survive the manager downside, and exactly when should it change?

**Short answer:** It survives as a conditional design direction, not as an investment case.

The downside assumes a `$21m` liquidity screen, two candidate validations, an independent `$3,900` fee sensitivity (`50% × $7,800`, not a selected two-account fee total), and 50 hours/month of productive-capacity hypothesis. None becomes recognized value. Federated coordination still addresses the no-regret need for common data ownership, control standards, decision lineage, local rights, staged integration, and reversible learning.

The recommendation changes to **local stabilization** if ACG cannot establish global data/control ownership, minimum integration readiness, or an affordable initial stage. A critical data, control, service, local-right, resilience, or rollback failure also blocks or narrows the federated design. The globally coordinated option remains on hold until legal/local, architecture, cyber/resilience, mobility, cost, and rollback evidence materially closes.

**Decision implication:** Confirm the switch/stop conditions now so that later evidence—not sunk cost or score attachment—determines the path.

**Evidence:** `W3_strategic_options.md`; `W3_business_case.md`; `W3_decision_log.md`.
