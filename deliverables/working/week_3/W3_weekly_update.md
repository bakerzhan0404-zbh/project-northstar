## Executive summary

The Week 3 evidence supports **federated coordination as the provisional design direction**, with local stabilization retained as a controlled fallback. Federated scores `87` versus `72` for local stabilization and `60` for the globally coordinated option, and leads all five plausible weight sensitivities. The operating model and two deterministic pilot frames are specific enough for readiness review, but all execution-evidence gates remain `OPEN`. The business model is a validation case only: recognized cash/P&L/capacity value is `$0`, risk is `NOT QUANTIFIED`, and actual costs, ROI, NPV, payback, and a funding recommendation are unavailable.

**Status is Amber** because the direction and evidence plan are clear, while client ownership, source readiness, control/service approvals, local/legal conditions, cost, and investment evidence remain unresolved. No pilot, production change, cash movement, closure, labor action, benefit, spend, procurement, or scale is approved.
