## I01 — Cash data and visibility foundation

**Outcome:** A governed daily position for all 55 supplied accounts, with source, receipt timestamp, balance type, owner, reconciliation, evidence layer, and exception lineage.

- **Scope:** Account/source master; expected account-day calendar; metric contract; source authentication; timestamp capture; daily reconciliation; exception ownership; ten-account design cohort followed by controlled expansion.
- **Excluded:** Cash movement, mobility certification, platform selection, or treating same-day proxy results as operational cutoff performance.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / Group Treasurer / Treasury Data Lead with CIO delegate.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** G0 evidence-mobilization authority; named account, source, data, and CIO owners; access to the 55-account/source inventory.
- **Milestones:** G1 population and metric contract; G2 source/control design; G3 cohort readiness and rollback; G4 approved read-only production; Month 6 stable daily operation.
- **Completion evidence:** Approved contract, all-55 census, reconciled run, owned exception log, access/audit/recovery tests, and KPI ownership.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Missing sources stay in denominator; unexplained material breaks stop funding decisions; uncertified mobility contributes zero value; prior-source fallback displays its limitations.
- **Exit:** Stable approved daily run for the in-scope population, with service/control thresholds met and BAU owner named.

## I02 — Liquidity certification and funding discipline

**Outcome:** Current account-level mobility, buffer, legal/tax, regulatory, service, timing, and economics evidence supports controlled funding decisions.

- **Scope:** Certification register; operating buffers; settlement/forecast events; permitted actions; delegated authority; decision evidence; transfer/no-action outcomes; periodic recertification.
- **Excluded:** Generalizing positive balance or the `$21m/$35m/$46.2m` screen into movable cash.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / Group Treasurer / Regional Treasury Leads.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** I01 reconciled population and source contract; named regional, legal, tax, regulatory, service, and Finance validators; G1 approval.
- **Milestones:** G1 population linkage; G2 certification design; G3 ready accounts only; G4 bounded approved action; G5 scaled funding discipline.
- **Completion evidence:** Current certifications, approved buffers, specialist sign-offs, decision and approval trail, transaction/no-action evidence, actual economics, Finance attribution.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Unresolved local context makes an account ineligible; dual approval; service and emergency rights; reconciled confirmation; no recognition before VG01–VG05.
- **Exit:** Sustained, controlled funding decisions with Finance-validated results and no critical service/control breach.

## I03 — Payment intake, controls, and exception reduction

**Outcome:** A controlled denominator, standard intake, reason-coded exception process, service protections, and bounded test reduce avoidable rework without removing required controls.

- **Scope:** Reconcile payment population; 120-record evidence review; required fields; source documents; cutoff/criticality; reason taxonomy; SoD/access; bank acknowledgement; ERP/bank reconciliation; emergency route; like-for-like measurement.
- **Excluded:** ACG-wide extrapolation from 7,600 records, broad automation without cause evidence, labor reduction, or production change during the NA freeze.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** Group Treasurer / Shared Services Lead / Payment Operations Lead.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** Controlled payment denominator and event/source access; named process and control owners; critical-payment rules and exact North America freeze calendar.
- **Milestones:** G1 denominator; Day 75 root-cause readout; G3 intervention/readiness decision; G4 bounded test outside freeze; G5 sustain/scale decision.
- **Completion evidence:** Population control, source/event linkage, approved intervention, control/service/rollback tests, comparable pre/post results, Finance-approved capacity treatment.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Critical flows protected; no silent repair loop; reason and owner required; emergency bypass cannot become standard; failed control/service/rollback triggers stop.
- **Exit:** Sustained improvement in the approved cohort without service/control deterioration, with root cause and attribution evidenced.

## I04 — Account rationalization validation

**Outcome:** Each approved closure has a complete local dependency, control, continuity, cost, and fee-removal evidence package.

- **Scope:** Validate AC0004, AC0009, AC0024, and AC0037; receipts/direct debits; linked services; signatories; purpose; regulatory/tax; continuity; closure charge; invoice follow-through.
- **Excluded:** A target of ten closures, bulk closure, or P&L recognition from the `$7,800` estimate alone.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / Group Treasurer / Regional Finance.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** Authoritative account inventory; local account owners; legal, tax, regulatory, service, continuity, closure-cost, and fee evidence.
- **Milestones:** G1 checklists and local owners; G2 go/no-go per account; G4 approved closures and confirmation; Month 6 fee verification.
- **Completion evidence:** Approved checklist, zero unresolved dependency, completed closure, reconciled residual items, actual invoices showing removal, Finance sign-off.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Collection, payroll, tax, regulatory, resilience, and local-service dependencies are explicit stop conditions; rollback/contingency defined before closure.
- **Exit:** Each account is either safely closed with verified net fee removal or retained with a documented purpose and review date.

## I05 — Staged integration, access, and resilience

**Outcome:** Existing ERPs and selected bank sources connect through secure, supportable, reversible interfaces with approved access, audit, continuity, and recovery.

- **Scope:** Authoritative-source and architecture map; interface tiers; identity/access; SoD; audit trail; monitoring; incident/support; test environments; recovery; ≤4-hour rollback; acquired-ERP transition awareness.
- **Excluded:** Major ERP replacement, vendor/product selection, or enterprise rollout before evidence.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / CIO / Enterprise Architecture and Cybersecurity.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** I01 source map and I03 use cases; architecture, cyber, access, support, and control owners; safe test environment and approved rollback method.
- **Milestones:** G1 source/owner map; G2 architecture/control design; G3 safe-environment rehearsal; G4 release approval; G5 scaled interfaces/support.
- **Completion evidence:** Approved architecture, threat/control evidence, access certification, auditability, reconciliation, recovery and rollback test, run-support model and recurring cost.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Least privilege, SoD, independent testing, release/change control, monitored fallbacks, data reconciliation, incident containment.
- **Exit:** Stable supported interfaces meet service/control thresholds and can recover without unreconciled data or critical-service loss.

## I06 — Governance, service, and adoption

**Outcome:** Enterprise policy and performance ownership operate with clear regional/local rights, service levels, emergency procedures, training, and change control.

- **Scope:** Steering Committee; Transformation Office; workstream forums; Treasury Operating Council; RACI; delegated authority; critical-service definitions; blackout calendar; training; communications; adoption; issue/escalation/change control.
- **Excluded:** Central policy as blanket removal of local execution or emergency authority.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / CFO and Group Treasurer / Transformation Director.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** G0 sponsor mandate; named global, regional, and local decision owners; approved local/emergency rights; service and blackout calendars; change support.
- **Milestones:** G0 governance activation; G1 RACI/rights; G2 service/emergency approval; G3 readiness; G4 hypercare; G5 scale; G6 BAU council.
- **Completion evidence:** Terms of reference, decision rights, meeting records, service procedures, training/access certification, adoption results, BAU handoff.
- **Primary risks/controls:** One accountable owner per decision; local dissent visible; service/control issues can stop the program; Internal Audit remains independent.
- **Exit:** BAU forums operate two full cycles with stable ownership, service, control, KPI, and escalation performance.

## I07 — Benefits, cost, and KPI assurance

**Outcome:** Finance recognizes value only from approved baselines, formulas, owners, timing, attribution, and realized evidence; one-time and recurring costs remain separately controlled.

- **Scope:** CR01–CR10; VG01–VG12; KPI dictionary; baseline/target approvals; benefit ledger; cost forecast; attribution; change control; monthly review; independent Finance sign-off.
- **Excluded:** Adding cash, P&L, capacity, and risk; monetizing capacity without redeployment; treating model-control passes as evidence; computing returns before required inputs exist.
- **Executive sponsor / accountable owner / lead:** CFO / CFO and Finance Benefits Lead / Finance Benefits Manager.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** Named initiative and Finance owners; KPI/source contracts; CR01–CR10 and VG01–VG12 evidence definitions; independent recognition approvers.
- **Milestones:** G1 baseline ownership; G2 cost evidence; G3 approved target logic; G4 initial tracking; G5 scale validation; G6 BAU ledger.
- **Completion evidence:** Closed relevant gates, approved sources/formulas, monthly actuals, variance explanations, attribution, Finance approval, audit trail.
- **Primary risks/controls:** Recognized value starts at `$0`; changes to population/formula/baseline require approval and restatement; recurring cost is visible; benefit owner cannot self-approve recognition.
- **Exit:** BAU Finance process produces reproducible monthly cost/value reporting with controlled changes and no category aggregation.
