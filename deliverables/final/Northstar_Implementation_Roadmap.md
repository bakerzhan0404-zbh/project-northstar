# Project Northstar — 90-Day Mobilization and 12–18 Month Roadmap

**Prepared by:** Baker
**Date:** 24 August 2026
**Status:** Final analyst recommendation for Steering Committee decision; execution authority not yet granted

## Roadmap thesis

ACG should spend the first 90 days making a later production decision evidence-ready. The program then scales only through separate control, service, affordability, value, and rollback gates. Day 90 is not a launch date.

## 12–18 month sequence

| Phase | Timing | Governing objective | In-scope outcomes | Exit decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobilization | Days 1–30 | Establish controlled facts and ownership | Reconciled populations; metric contracts; owners; freeze calendar; decision rights | Proceed, narrow, or extend evidence |
| Evidence and design | Days 31–60 | Prove local, control, service, architecture, and affordability conditions | Certification and control designs; cost ranges; remediation owners; fallback viability | Proceed, switch to local stabilization, or stop |
| Decision readiness | Days 61–90 | Make a later bounded test safe and measurable | Locked baselines/targets; cohort evidence; rollback; production/funding decision requirements | Stop, extend, or authorize a separate bounded pilot |
| Wave 1 | Months 4–6 | Deliver controlled visibility and targeted no-regret improvement | Approved read-only visibility; governed daily position; bounded payment change outside freeze; validated low-risk closures; live KPI/benefit controls | Launch/rollback at G4; sustain or redesign |
| Wave 2 | Months 7–12 | Expand only where Wave 1 evidence holds | Broader certified visibility; liquidity decision discipline; priority payment standardization; selected interfaces; training and support | Scale, hold, or redesign at G5 |
| Scale and BAU | Months 13–18 | Embed the federated model and hand off control | Stable operating council; support and control testing; approved manual-work retirement; benefits assurance; acquisition-ready standards | Accept BAU or extend at G6 |

## Seven stage gates

| Gate | Timing | Minimum non-compensating evidence | Decision owner | Allowed decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 | Day 0 | Direction, owners, ceiling role, local fallback, and no-execution boundary recorded | CFO / Steering Committee | Authorize evidence work, revise, or stop |
| G1 | Day 30 | Controlled populations, sources, definitions, owners, calendars, and baseline gaps | Group Treasurer / CIO / Shared Services | Proceed, narrow, or extend |
| G2 | Day 60 | Local rights, controls, architecture, resilience, cost ranges, and remediation ownership | CFO / CIO / Control owners | Proceed, switch, or stop |
| G3 | Day 90 | Baselines, target rules, cohort readiness, closed critical gates, rollback, freeze/sign-off path | CFO / Steering Committee | Stop, extend, or authorize a separate bounded pilot |
| G4 | Months 4–6 | Funding and production approvals, control/service pass, outside applicable freeze | CFO / CIO / BU Finance | Launch, delay, or rollback |
| G5 | Months 9–12 | Sustained like-for-like outcomes, validated benefits, service/control pass, refreshed costs | Steering Committee | Scale, hold, or redesign |
| G6 | Months 15–18 | Stable ownership, procedures, support, control testing, risk acceptance, benefit handoff | CFO / Group Treasurer / CIO | Accept BAU or extend |

No average readiness score can override a failed critical gate.

## Dependency logic

1. I06 and I07 begin immediately because governance and value controls govern every other workstream.
2. I01 supplies the controlled cash population and definitions required by I02.
3. I03 may complete evidence and safe design during the North America freeze, but no North America routing or approval-workflow production change may occur inside it.
4. I05 is a release gate for any interface, access, or workflow production change; it does not imply platform procurement.
5. I04 can move fastest because it is bounded, but no closure occurs without local dependencies, continuity, and fee evidence.
6. Scale requires G5: sustained like-for-like evidence, not a successful technical deployment alone.

## Program critical path

**Owner confirmation → population/metric control → local/control/architecture review → bottom-up cost evidence → target and cohort readiness → rollback rehearsal → G3 decision → separate funding/production approval → Wave 1 → sustained evidence → G5 scale.**

Any delay in critical evidence moves the later milestones right. It does not justify weakening the gate.

## North America freeze rule

North America will not accept payment-routing or approval-workflow production changes during the confirmed eight weeks surrounding peak holiday operations. The exact calendar remains to be supplied by NA BU Finance, and any North America production change requires Rachel Kim's sign-off. Data cleanup, design, testing, and low-risk account validation may proceed. Until the exact calendar is confirmed, **Month 5 is the earliest realistic North America production-start planning assumption**.

## Roadmap change control

- The Transformation Director owns the integrated baseline.
- Workstream leads may manage tasks within approved scope and tolerance.
- Any change affecting a critical gate, production population, local right, service threshold, control, rollback, cost ceiling, benefit formula, or committed date enters the change log.
- The Steering Committee approves scope/funding/wave changes; Finance approves benefit-ledger changes; the accountable control owner approves control acceptance; the relevant BU owner approves service/production exceptions.
- If the federated foundation cannot close by G2 or G3, use local stabilization with the common data/control minimum rather than allowing an indefinite “amber” program.
