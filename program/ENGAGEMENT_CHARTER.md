# Engagement Charter

## Engagement identity

- **Engagement:** Project Northstar
- **Client:** Aurelius Consumer Group
- **Sponsor:** Elena Marquez, Group CFO
- **Primary client lead:** Daniel Wu, Group Treasurer
- **Consulting role:** Analyst, J.P. Morgan Payments Advisory
- **Duration:** One month / four weekly phases
- **Submission cadence:** One integrated assignment and status update per week
- **Decision date:** End-of-Week-4 steering committee

## Executive question

> How should ACG redesign its global treasury operating model to improve cash visibility, strengthen liquidity control, reduce avoidable cost and manual effort, and create a scalable platform for growth—without introducing unacceptable implementation or control risk?

## Scope

### In scope

- Bank-account and legal-entity footprint
- Cash visibility and cash-position reporting
- Liquidity concentration and idle/deficit cash
- Payments execution and exception handling
- Receivables reconciliation at a diagnostic level
- Foreign-exchange transaction patterns at a diagnostic level
- Treasury organization, governance, controls, data, and technology
- Business case, sequencing, change risks, and KPIs

### Out of scope

- Product selection or vendor procurement
- Legal, tax, accounting, sanctions, or regulatory advice
- Detailed system architecture or implementation coding
- Bank pricing negotiation
- Credit underwriting
- Claims about actual JPMorgan client recommendations or proprietary capabilities

## Required consulting answer

The final answer must contain five linked components:

1. **Diagnosis:** What is happening, where, and how material is it?
2. **Root causes:** Why is it happening?
3. **Strategic choice:** What should management do, and what should it not do now?
4. **Value case:** What benefits are plausible, what assumptions drive them, and what could erode them?
5. **Execution:** Who does what, in what sequence, with which controls and success measures?

## Rules of engagement

- Treat all client data as confidential.
- Maintain clear source attribution for ACG data, analyst calculations, and external benchmarks.
- Cite every external statistic in slide footnotes and the source register.
- Separate facts, calculations, assumptions, and judgments.
- Do not recommend a named banking product merely because it appears in public source material.
- Escalate material data-quality issues rather than silently correcting them.
- Use ranges for uncertain benefits and show the underlying calculation.
- Maintain a decision log and version-controlled analytical files.
- Lead every update with the implication for the client decision.

## Definition of success

The engagement succeeds if a skeptical CFO can understand the recommendation in five minutes, trace every major claim to evidence, see the financial and operational tradeoffs, and know what should happen in the first 90 days.
