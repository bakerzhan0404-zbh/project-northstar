# Evaluation Rubric

Total: 100 points.

| Dimension | Weight | Excellent performance |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem structuring | 12 | Decision-led issue tree is MECE enough for action; hypotheses evolve with evidence. |
| Analytical rigor | 18 | Calculations reconcile, definitions are explicit, uncertainty is tested, and code is reproducible. |
| Treasury understanding | 12 | Correctly connects liquidity, accounts, payments, data, controls, organization, and technology. |
| Insight quality | 12 | Prioritizes material root causes and converts facts into implications rather than reporting observations. |
| Recommendation | 15 | Makes clear choices, addresses tradeoffs, and follows directly from ACG evidence and constraints. |
| Business case | 10 | Separates cash, P&L, capacity, and risk; shows ranges, timing, assumptions, and ownership. |
| Implementation realism | 9 | Roadmap includes dependencies, governance, controls, change management, and measurable milestones. |
| Communication | 8 | Executive materials are concise, answer-first, well sourced, and defensible in Q&A. |
| Professional documentation | 4 | Evidence pack is current, traceable, organized, and suitable for handoff. |

## Performance bands

- **90–100 — Outstanding:** Performs at a strong analyst level with minimal correction.
- **80–89 — Strong:** Clear and credible work with limited gaps in depth or polish.
- **70–79 — Developing:** Sound direction, but material analytical or communication gaps remain.
- **Below 70 — Rework required:** Recommendation is not yet sufficiently supported or executable.

## Automatic quality deductions

- Up to 10 points: unsupported or misrepresented external claim
- Up to 10 points: calculation cannot be reproduced
- Up to 8 points: incorrect or misleading source attribution
- Up to 8 points: recommendation omits material control or local-market constraints
- Up to 5 points: missing source, assumption, or decision documentation
