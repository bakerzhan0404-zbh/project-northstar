# Stakeholder Evidence Pack

> These interview excerpts contain perceptions, inconsistencies, and potential bias by design.

## 1. Elena Marquez — Group CFO

“I do not need a catalog of treasury products. I need to know which decisions improve control and release value, and which decisions we are not ready to make.”

- Wants credible benefits inside 12 months.
- Believes excess complexity accumulated through acquisition.
- Is willing to centralize policy but not create operational fragility.
- Will challenge any benefit estimate that lacks a named owner or baseline.

## 2. Daniel Wu — Group Treasurer

“My team spends the beginning of each week assembling a global position. By the time we reconcile it, parts of it are already stale.”

- Group Treasury has seven employees.
- Estimates that 60–70% of global cash is visible by the start of each business day, but has not documented the calculation.
- Supports greater centralization and automation.
- Suspects at least ten accounts could close, subject to local validation.

## 3. Priya Nair — APAC Regional Controller

“Headquarters calls balances ‘idle’ without recognizing local settlement timing, regulatory restrictions, or the need to protect supplier relationships.”

- Runs a daily local cash process.
- Believes local teams need defined autonomy for urgent payments.
- Notes that some accounts serve regulatory or collection purposes.
- Is concerned a global model will apply U.S. assumptions to APAC markets.

## 4. Lucas Schneider — EMEA Shared Services Lead

“The largest source of rework is not the bank platform. It is incomplete invoice and beneficiary information arriving from the business.”

- Tracks payment exceptions manually.
- Reports recurring repair activity for cross-border wires and rush payments.
- Wants standard intake fields and earlier cutoff discipline.
- Thinks payment format standardization could materially improve straight-through processing.

## 5. Sarah Coleman — Chief Information Officer

“We can connect banks and ERPs, but first tell me which data is authoritative and who owns it.”

- Will not approve a new platform without data ownership and control requirements.
- Prefers staged integration around existing ERPs.
- Warns that one acquired ERP instance will be retired in approximately two years.
- Requires cybersecurity, access, and resilience review before implementation.

## 6. Martin Blake — Internal Audit Director

“Standardization can improve control, but centralization also concentrates failure risk.”

- Has observed inconsistent user-access recertification.
- Wants evidence that emergency-payment procedures are controlled.
- Expects segregation of duties and audit trails to be explicit in the future state.
- Will not accept efficiency benefits that assume removal of required controls.

## 7. Rachel Kim — North America Business Unit CFO

“We support better visibility, but the transformation cannot slow customer refunds or critical supplier payments during peak season.”

- Favors pilots outside the fourth-quarter peak.
- Wants business service levels in the design.
- Questions whether every low-balance account deserves immediate attention.

## Interview synthesis task

For each statement, you should record:

- Observation or claim
- Evidence required
- Implication if true
- Contradictory evidence
- Stakeholder interest or potential bias
- Follow-up question

Do not treat stakeholder confidence as proof.
