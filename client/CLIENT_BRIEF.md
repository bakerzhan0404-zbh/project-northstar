# Confidential Client Brief — Aurelius Consumer Group

## Company overview

Aurelius Consumer Group (ACG) is a privately held U.S.-headquartered producer and distributor of premium home, personal-care, and wellness products. It sells through retailers, distributors, and direct-to-consumer channels. Over the last six years, ACG expanded through four acquisitions and now operates legal entities in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

## Why the CFO initiated Northstar

ACG met its earnings target in FY2025, yet the CFO is concerned that treasury is operating as a collection of local practices rather than an enterprise capability. Three events increased urgency:

1. A European entity used a short-term borrowing facility while other entities held material surplus cash.
2. A supplier payment was released after cutoff and required manual intervention to avoid a production disruption.
3. The board asked how much cash ACG can reliably mobilize within 24 hours; management could not provide a reconciled answer on the same day.

The CFO does not want a technology-led answer. She wants a fact-based operating-model recommendation with clear economics, risks, prerequisites, and sequencing.

## Current-state observations supplied by management

- Local finance teams maintain many bank relationships and spreadsheets.
- Group Treasury produces a consolidated cash-position report each Tuesday.
- Several accounts were inherited through acquisitions and may no longer be necessary.
- Payment files use multiple formats and approval practices vary by entity.
- Intercompany funding decisions are largely reactive.
- Cash forecasting is performed by local teams using inconsistent definitions.
- ERP and bank-reference data are not consistently standardized.
- Management believes automation will help, but data quality and ownership are unresolved.

These are starting observations, not proven conclusions. You must validate, quantify, and challenge them.

## Client objectives

The CFO has asked for a recommendation that could plausibly:

- Deliver a reliable daily view of global cash.
- Improve access to and control over liquidity.
- Reduce avoidable borrowing and idle cash.
- Lower manual treasury and payment effort.
- Improve payment traceability and reconciliation.
- Establish consistent ownership and control standards.
- Support future acquisitions without multiplying complexity.

## Constraints

- No major ERP replacement during the next 18 months.
- Local regulatory, tax, and business-continuity requirements may limit account consolidation.
- FY2026 transformation funding is limited; initiatives must be prioritized.
- The business cannot tolerate material payment disruption during peak season.
- Local finance leaders fear loss of responsiveness under centralization.
- The CFO requires measurable benefits within 12 months, even if the full program takes longer.

## Steering-committee decision required at the end of Week 4

The committee must decide whether to authorize a 90-day mobilization and, if so:

- Which transformation ambition to pursue
- Which initiatives enter Wave 1
- What funding envelope and benefits range to validate
- What governance and control conditions must be satisfied
- Which decisions should be deferred pending legal, tax, or technical assessment

## Analytical questions

You should answer at least the following:

1. How fragmented is the account and bank footprint, and where is complexity concentrated?
2. How much cash appears idle, restricted, operationally required, or offset by simultaneous deficits?
3. How frequent and costly are overdraft-like positions and avoidable external funding?
4. Which payment processes create the most exceptions, delays, and manual effort?
5. How complete and timely is treasury's visibility?
6. Which problems arise from process, data, governance, organization, or technology?
7. What future-state design principles should guide ACG?
8. Which initiatives deliver the best balance of value, feasibility, control, and speed?
9. What dependencies and risks determine the sequence?
10. How should management measure adoption, control, and realized value?

## Deliverable boundaries

Use public JPMorgan material to establish industry context and illustrate relevant patterns. Do not copy a published client solution into ACG's recommendation. ACG's answer must be derived from ACG evidence, explicit assumptions, and the constraints above.
