# Welcome to Project Northstar

**To:** Baker  
**From:** Engagement Manager, Project Northstar  
**Subject:** Your assignment — Aurelius Consumer Group treasury transformation

Baker,

Welcome to Project Northstar. Over the next month, you will serve as the analyst on a J.P. Morgan Payments Advisory engagement for Aurelius Consumer Group (ACG), a multinational consumer-products company.

ACG has grown faster than its treasury infrastructure. Its leadership has concerns about cash visibility, fragmented bank accounts, manual payments, inconsistent controls, and the ability to mobilize liquidity across markets. The CFO has asked our team for a decision-ready recommendation—not a generic treasury report or a catalog of technology.

Your responsibility is to establish the facts, challenge assumptions, evaluate strategic choices, quantify a credible value range, and show how the client could execute. You will work with imperfect data and conflicting stakeholder perspectives. This is intentional. Professional consulting requires you to manage uncertainty without hiding it or allowing it to stop progress.

Your work will be judged on the quality of your reasoning, not on whether you arrive at a predetermined answer. A strong recommendation will be:

- Relevant to the CFO's decision
- Supported by traceable evidence
- Explicit about assumptions and limitations
- Balanced across value, risk, feasibility, and control
- Specific enough to implement
- Clear enough for an executive to understand quickly

Begin with the engagement charter and client brief. Do not open your analysis by searching for a solution. First establish the decision, structure the problem, and determine what evidence you need.

You will submit one integrated assignment at the end of each week. At the end of Week 4, you will present to the client steering committee. Expect challenge. Your objective is not to have an instant answer to every question; it is to demonstrate command of the evidence, distinguish fact from judgment, and identify the correct follow-up when certainty is not yet possible.

Good luck. We look forward to your recommendation.

— Project Northstar Engagement Manager
